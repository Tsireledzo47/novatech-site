Why is my Wi‑Fi not working? Ensure Wi‑Fi is enabled, reconnect to the correct network (e.g., NovaTech‑Staff), restart your device, and test another network service.

Policy Library
Official governance for support, escalation and priority handling.

Escalation Policy

Issues are escalated when they exceed the target response time, impact multiple users, cause significant business disruption, or require specialist network or system administration.

  1. The Service Desk investigates and categorizes the issue.
  2. The Team Lead reviews overdue or high‑impact incidents.
  3. Network/System Specialists handle infrastructure‑level faults.
  4. Management is notified in cases of major service disruption.
