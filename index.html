Knowledge Base — Self-Service Solutions
The Knowledge Base is a centralized self-service library designed to empower users to resolve common IT issues independently without logging a support ticket. Each article explains what the issue means, common causes, symptoms, and step-by-step professional fix to save time and improve productivity.

💻 SOFTWARE — Application Crashes & Errors

What this means: Software is any program like Office, Outlook, Teams. Crash occurs when program closes unexpectedly or shows error message and stops working completely.

Professional fix: Record exact error message, restart application, check for updates in Help menu, restart computer. If still failing, log ticket with screenshot for Tier 1 support to investigate further.

🌐 NETWORK — Wi-Fi, Internet & Connectivity

What this means: Network issues relate to connection to internet or company Wi-Fi. Symptoms include "No Internet", "Connected but no internet", or pages not loading at all.

Professional fix: Check Wi-Fi icon status, turn Wi-Fi off and on, select correct network NovaTech-5G, enter correct password, restart router unplug 30 seconds then plug back wait 2 minutes for green lights.

🔐 ACCESS — Shared Folders & Permissions

What this means: Access issues occur when you try to open shared drive and get "Access Denied" or "You do not have permission" message blocking you.

Professional fix: Ensure you are on company network or VPN connected, verify path like \\fileserver\finance is correct, confirm with HR if you should have access, submit Access Request ticket with manager approval.

🖨️ HARDWARE — Printer & Peripherals

What this means: Hardware covers physical devices like printers, scanners, monitors. Printer not printing or showing offline is common issue in office environment.

Professional fix: Check printer power and network cable, go to Settings > Devices > Printers > Add Printer > Select your printer from list, install driver, check toner and paper tray, print test page.

📧 EMAIL — Outlook Not Working

What this means: Email problems include Outlook not sending or receiving, stuck in offline mode, or password prompts repeatedly appearing blocking work.

Professional fix: Check internet connection first, open Outlook check bottom bar if offline click Work Offline to disable, check password expired, restart Outlook, try webmail at outlook.office.com to confirm account works.

🔑 PASSWORD — Account Locked or Forgotten

What this means: Password issues include forgotten password, account locked after 3 wrong attempts, or password expired after 90 days per security policy.

Professional fix: On login screen click Forgot Password > Enter work email > Check inbox for reset link > Create new strong password with uppercase, lowercase, number, symbol, 8+ characters, do not share.

Our Departments
This section explains core departments that work together to deliver efficient IT services. Each department has distinct responsibilities but collaborates to ensure seamless support, budget control and people management. Below are detailed roles of each department for understanding organizational structure.

🏢 IT Department

Technical operations and helpdesk support — the backbone of NovaTech Solutions, responsible for keeping all systems running 24/7 with high availability.

The IT Department is responsible for overall infrastructure management including servers, networks, firewalls, switches, wireless access points and UPS systems. It manages system integration between different business applications, ensures cybersecurity through firewalls, antivirus and access controls, provides helpdesk support via ticket system from Tier 1 to Tier 3, monitors performance through dashboards and graphs, and maintains asset register to track equipment lifecycle, age, warranty and replacement planning for budgeting.

👥 HR Department

People and access management — focusing on human capital, employee experience, compliance and organizational development for staff wellbeing.

The HR Department focuses on talent acquisition, recruitment and onboarding of IT staff and other employees. It manages employee access requests for shared folders, emails and systems, oversees training and development programs, handles leave, performance management and disciplinary processes, ensures compliance with labour laws and company policies, and coordinates with IT for account creation, password resets, role-based access and access revocation when employees join or leave.

💰 Finance Department

Asset and budget control — ensuring financial health, cost-effective IT operations, procurement governance and value for money across all IT spending.

The Finance Department oversees budgeting, procurement and asset valuation for all IT equipment and software licenses. It tracks total asset balance, calculates depreciation and total cost of ownership, approves purchases for hardware and software based on business case, manages vendor payments and contracts, controls IT expenditure against budget, and works with IT to prioritize asset replacement based on age, warranty expiry, business impact and risk to optimize costs and avoid failures.

ASSET MOTIVATION

Asset Motivation is the documented business justification for why an organization acquires, tracks, maintains, or disposes of an IT asset. It answers: Why do we have this asset? What business value does it provide? What happens if it fails?

Without clear motivation, you cannot justify the R97,900 portfolio cost, prioritize maintenance, or make replacement decisions. Every asset must be linked to a business driver: Cost Control, Productivity, Security, Compliance, or Operational Continuity.

LIFECYCLE RULE

Asset Age = 2025 - Purchase Year

IF Age > 5 Years → REPLACE (End-of-Life)
IF Age ≤ 5 Years → RETAIN (In Warranty / Efficient)
