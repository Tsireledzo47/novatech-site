Our Departments
This section explains core departments that work together to deliver efficient IT services. Each department has distinct responsibilities but collaborates to ensure seamless support.

🏢 IT Department

Technical operations and helpdesk support — the backbone of NovaTech Solutions.

The IT Department is responsible for overall infrastructure management including servers, networks, firewalls, switches and wireless access points. It manages system integration between different business applications, ensures cybersecurity through firewalls and antivirus, provides helpdesk support via ticket system, monitors performance through dashboards, and maintains asset register to track equipment lifecycle and replacement planning.

👥 HR Department

People and access management — focusing on human capital and employee experience.

The HR Department focuses on talent acquisition, recruitment and onboarding of IT staff and other employees. It manages employee access requests for shared folders, emails and systems, oversees training and development, handles leave and performance management, ensures compliance with labour laws, and coordinates with IT for account creation, password resets and access revocation when employees join or leave.

💰 Finance Department

Asset and budget control — ensuring financial health and cost-effective IT operations.

The Finance Department oversees budgeting, procurement and asset valuation for all IT equipment and software licenses. It tracks total asset balance, calculates depreciation and total cost of ownership, approves purchases for hardware and software, manages vendor payments, controls IT expenditure, and works with IT to prioritize asset replacement based on age, warranty expiry and business impact to optimize costs.
